Different test / qualifying merger

Section 138.2 does not equal a blanket tax exemption.

Qualifying transfers require the statute's conditions, including all-property transfer, beneficiary consideration solely in the continuing fund interest, resident trustee and timely election. Deemed tax-year ends and ACB/other reporting controls still matter.

05
The confirmation register

The questions that unlock a viable plan.

A small selection from the existing research playbook, grouped by the functions that must confirm the actual route.

Legal / regulatory
  • Are the terminating and continuing entities related segregated-fund trusts, an underlying mutual fund, or a policy-level substitution?
  • Which issued contracts and provinces govern the notice, election, fee and withdrawal rights? Does the final 2025 guidance align with contract wording and local implementation?
  • Who is actually entitled to vote or elect: mutual-fund unitholders, insurance policyowners, annuitants or another party?
Tax / reporting
  • Is a §138.2 qualifying transfer possible; who makes the election, when, and what are the pre-close trust year-end and property consequences?
  • Which income/gains/losses should be allocated before the effective date, and how will T3, RL-16, ACB and post-merger slip reconciliation work?
  • Which client cohorts are non-registered, registered or subject to different contractual tax treatment?
Product / actuarial
  • What happens to death and maturity guarantees, reset history, guarantee bases, anniversaries, issue ages, beneficiaries and grandfathered options?
  • Are investment objective, risk, fund category, FER, separate insurance charges and surrender charges comparable by series and contract cohort?
  • Which available “similar” funds and choices can actually be offered to the client?
Fund accounting / operations
  • What is the NAV/unit exchange design across valuation times, multi-series pricing, suspended activity and fractional unit handling?
  • How are holdings, broker/dealer feeds, future contributions, pending trades, reconciliations and prior-year reporting preserved?
  • Which maker-checker attestations and before/after client-level controls establish correct value and guarantees?
Client communications / vendors
  • Can each notice clearly show what moves, what stays, tax effects, fees, choices and deadlines without promising an unapproved outcome?
  • How are servicing intermediaries, owner data, alternate-format needs, mailing evidence, elections and rejected/late responses tracked?
  • Which vendor, dealer and insurer dependencies create the actual critical path?
